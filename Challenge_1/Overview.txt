Shirogane Noel has decided to establish an Adventurer Guild in the Hololive universe!

As the guild's developer, you've been tasked with building a simple system that manages guild members, their abilities, and their participation in quests.

However, different Hololive members have different abilities. Noel is a knight, Flare is an archer, and Pekora is a mischievous rabbit who specializes in explosives.

Your job is to use structs, interfaces, and errors to create a flexible system that supports different adventurers.

1.  Create structs

1. Knight (Shirogane Noel)

Fields:
- Name (string)
- Health (int)
- AttackPower (int)
- Armor (int)
2. Archer (Shiranui Flare)

Fields:
- Name (string)
- Health (int)
- AttackPower (int)
- Arrows (int)
